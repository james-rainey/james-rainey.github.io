About

My research combines computer vision with practical system development. I work on embedded food monitoring, satellite-based algae detection and retinal vessel segmentation, with experience spanning model development, hardware integration and evaluation.

I’m also involved in the JPEG Trust standard, which provides a framework for assessing trust in digital media. Explore OpenJPT to learn more about JPEG Trust tools.

Outside of work I’m into hands-on technology and making things: 3D printing, Raspberry Pi projects and other maker activities that let me prototype ideas and tinker with hardware.

Research areas

All projects →

Food Recognition

Systems that recognise, segment and monitor food on embedded hardware.

Algae Detection

Monitoring harmful algal blooms by fusing radar and optical satellite imagery.

Medical Imaging

Segmenting retinal blood vessels in OCTA scans to support treatment of diabetic eye disease.

Neuromorphic Vision

Brain-inspired spiking neural networks running on low-power FPGAs.

Media Authenticity

Detecting manipulated images and verifying where visual content came from.

Gait Recognition

Identifying people by how they walk using markerless 3D motion capture.
