About

My main focus is trust in digital media. I contribute to the JPEG Trust standard, an international framework for establishing whether images and video can be trusted, and I work on turning it into practical tools. Explore OpenJPT to learn more about JPEG Trust tools.

I am also developing satellite-based monitoring of harmful algal blooms, fusing radar and optical imagery so that blooms in Lough Neagh can be tracked whatever the weather. The work is now being expanded to other lakes.

I am completing research on segmenting retinal blood vessels in OCTA scans, which aims to improve how diabetic macular ischaemia and oedema are assessed and treated.

Across these projects, and earlier work such as embedded food monitoring, I combine computer vision with practical system development, from model design to hardware integration and evaluation.

Outside of work I’m into hands-on technology and making things: 3D printing, Raspberry Pi projects and other maker activities that let me prototype ideas and tinker with hardware.
