Attended the first [JPEG Trust Summit](https://jpeg.org/items/20260828_JPEG_Trust_Summit_announcement.html) at The Alan Turing Institute in London on 12–13 November. The summit brought together experts from academia, industry, government, cultural heritage and standards bodies to discuss legal frameworks, real-world use and adoption of the [JPEG Trust](https://jpeg.org/jpegtrust/) standards for media provenance and authenticity.
